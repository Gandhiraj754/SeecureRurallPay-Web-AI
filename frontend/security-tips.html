⚠️ SCAMS HAPPENING NOW — STAY ALERT

📞 Fake KYC Call Scam VERY COMMON
Scammer calls saying your bank account will be closed unless you complete KYC. They ask for your OTP, card number, or to install a remote app.
  • Your bank will NEVER call asking for OTP or PIN
  • Hang up immediately and call your bank's official number
  • Never install apps someone asks you to install by phone
💸 UPI Money Request Scam VERY COMMON
Scammer sends a UPI COLLECT REQUEST saying "Enter your PIN to receive money." Entering your PIN on a collect request SENDS money — it does NOT receive it!
  • To receive money, you NEVER need to enter your PIN
  • PIN is only needed when YOU are sending money
  • Decline all unknown collect requests immediately
🔗 Fake Bank Website Link RISING
Scammers create websites that look exactly like your bank's website. They send the link via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Always check the URL — real bank: sbi.co.in, Fake: sbi-login.net
  • Use Check a Website Link in this app before clicking any link
  • Never enter your PIN/password on websites received via SMS
📲 Screen Share / AnyDesk Scam DANGER
Scammer asks you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer and share your screen "to help you." Once you share, they can see everything — your bank apps, OTPs, PINs.
  • NEVER share your screen with a stranger
  • Delete AnyDesk/TeamViewer if a stranger asked you to install it
  • Change your PIN and OTP immediately if you shared your screen
🎁 Lottery / Prize Scam
Message says you won a prize, lottery, or government money. To receive it, you must pay a small "tax" or "processing fee."
  • No real lottery requires you to pay to receive the prize
  • Government schemes never ask for money via UPI
  • Delete such messages immediately

✅ SAFE HABITS — PROTECT YOURSELF

🔑 Strong PIN Rules DO THIS
  • Never use your birthday, phone number, or 1234 as PIN
  • Use a different PIN for UPI, ATM, and phone lock
  • Change your UPI PIN every 3 months
  • Never share PIN with anyone — even family or bank staff
📱 Safe Phone Habits DO THIS
  • Always lock your phone with a PIN or fingerprint
  • Do not let strangers use your banking apps
  • Log out of banking apps after use on shared phones
  • Disable WhatsApp OTP auto-read on banking apps
  • Keep your phone's software updated
🌐 Safe Internet Habits DO THIS
  • Avoid doing banking on public Wi-Fi (tea stalls, stations)
  • Always check links before clicking — use our Link Checker
  • Only download banking apps from official app stores
  • Never save your banking password in a browser

🆘 IF YOU GOT SCAMMED — ACT FAST

⚡ Act within 30 minutes — you can recover the money!
  • Step 1: Call 1930 — National Cybercrime Helpline (24x7, Free)
  • Step 2: Call your bank's helpline immediately
  • Step 3: File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
  • Step 4: Block your UPI and card from your banking app
  • Step 5: Change all your PINs and passwords
